# Service desk operations

Synthetic workload analysis for a multi-site, thousands-of-users environment.

`analyse-tickets.py` consumes the enterprise lab's synthetic support tickets and summarises volume by priority and category plus escalation demand.

The purpose is to demonstrate operational thinking: identify recurring demand, separate P1/P2 work from routine requests, look for escalation patterns, and find candidates for automation or root-cause work.

No real service-desk records are included.
