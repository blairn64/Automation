# Scale and Evidence

## Synthetic estate

- 5,000 users
- 4 manufacturing sites
- Multiple departments and operational roles
- 15,000 synthetic sign-in events
- 2,500 synthetic support tickets
- Factory telemetry generated separately by `factory-lab/`

## Why this matters

The point of the lab is to demonstrate the engineering behaviour that appears when a support environment is no longer a handful of machines: filtering, batching, structured reporting, escalation, queue decoupling, monitoring and failure-domain isolation.

The numbers are synthetic test dimensions. They must never be described as measured production metrics.

## CV mapping

This lab supports demonstration of:

- enterprise-scale user administration patterns
- Microsoft-style identity telemetry analysis
- multi-site manufacturing operations
- IT/OT data-flow reasoning
- service-desk and escalation modelling
- operational reporting and automation

The actual professional experience remains the source of truth for the employment claims on the CV; this lab demonstrates the underlying technical practices at reproducible scale.
